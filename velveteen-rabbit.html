📚 Book of the Month (July)

The Velveteen
Rabbit

By Margery Williams

Genre: Classic · Friendship · Emotional Growth Age Group: 4+ years

Published in 1922, The Velveteen Rabbit tells the timeless story of a little toy rabbit who dreams of becoming "real" through the love of a child. As the Rabbit experiences friendship, loss, courage, and unconditional love, children discover that what truly matters isn't how perfect something looks—but how deeply it is loved.

Creative Learning Activities

Bring the story to life through imaginative play and meaningful conversations.

🧸

Create Your Own Story Companion

Using felt, paper, or socks, create your own little rabbit (or favourite toy). Give it a name, a personality, and imagine the adventures you'll share together.

💌

Love Makes Us Real

Draw or write about someone (or something) that makes you feel loved. It could be a family member, a friend, a pet—or even a favourite toy.

🎭

Toy Comes Alive

Choose your favourite stuffed toy and act out a day where it secretly comes alive whenever nobody is watching. What adventures would it have?

Discussion Points & Themes

The Velveteen Rabbit is a gentle classic that explores love, identity, change, and what it truly means to be real.

❤️

What Makes Someone Real?

Can love change how we see people and things? What do you think it means to be "real"?

🤝

Friendship

How did the Rabbit become the Boy's best friend? What makes a good friend?

🌱

Growing Up

Sometimes growing means changing. Is change always a bad thing?

🌟

Why This Book?

A gentle classic that reminds children that kindness, love, and memories are what make life meaningful. It's one of those rare books that stays with readers long after the final page.

Meet Margery Williams

Beloved Storyteller

Margery Williams was an English-American author best known for The Velveteen Rabbit. First published over 100 years ago, her timeless story continues to remind children and adults that love is what gives life its deepest meaning.
