📚 Book of the Month (August)

Malgudi
Schooldays

By R.K. Narayan

Classic / Adventure / Friendship Age 7+ Years

Step into the charming town of Malgudi and experience childhood through the eyes of Swami and his friends. Filled with school adventures, mischievous moments, unforgettable friendships, and everyday discoveries, Malgudi Schooldays captures the innocence and joy of growing up in India. A timeless classic that continues to delight readers across generations.

Creative Learning Activities

Bring the world of Malgudi to life through imagination, storytelling, and creative play.

🚂

Build Your Own Malgudi

Draw or create your own little town inspired by Malgudi. Add a school, railway station, river, playground, and the places where your own adventures would happen.

✉️

A Letter to Swami

Imagine Swami is your pen pal. Write him a letter telling him about your school, your friends, and what childhood looks like today.

🎒

School Then & Now

Talk to your parents or grandparents about their school days. Compare them with yours. What's changed? What has stayed the same?

🎭

Adventure Around the Corner

Create a short story about an ordinary day that suddenly turns into an unforgettable adventure — just like Swami's.

Discussion Points & Themes

Malgudi Schooldays is a timeless Indian classic that celebrates friendship, curiosity, imagination, and the simple joys of childhood.

🤝

Friendship

Swami, Mani, and Rajam share many adventures together. What makes a friendship strong, even when friends are different?

🌱

Growing Up

Childhood is full of mistakes, lessons, and discoveries. What have you learned from your own adventures?

🏫

School Life

School is about much more than books. What are your favourite memories of school?

🌟

Why This Book?

A timeless Indian classic that celebrates friendship, curiosity, imagination, and the simple joys of childhood — a reminder that life's greatest adventures often happen in the most ordinary moments.

Vocabulary List

Introduce these beautiful words while reading together.

🏡

Malgudi

The fictional town where Swami's adventures take place.

Malgudi is home to Swami and his friends throughout the story.

🚂

Adventure

An exciting or unusual experience filled with discovery.

Swami and his friends encounter many small adventures throughout their school days.

🤝

Companion

A friend who shares experiences and adventures with you.

Mani and Rajam are two of Swami's closest companions.

🌿

Curiosity

The desire to learn, explore, and discover new things.

Swami's curiosity often leads him into mischief and discovery.

Meet R.K. Narayan

Beloved Storyteller

R.K. Narayan was one of India's most celebrated authors, best known for bringing the fictional town of Malgudi to life.

Through simple yet powerful storytelling, he captured the humour, innocence, and everyday beauty of childhood and Indian life.

1906–2001 Indian Literary Legend
